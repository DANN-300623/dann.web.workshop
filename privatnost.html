Politika privatnosti

Poslednja izmena: oktobar 2026.

DANN.web.workshop poštuje Vašu privatnost. Ova stranica objašnjava koje podatke prikupljamo preko kontakt forme, zašto i kako ih koristimo — jednostavno, bez pravnog žargona.

Ko prikuplja podatke

Podatke prikuplja i njima upravlja DANN.web.workshop (dannwebworkshop.rs). Za sva pitanja o Vašim podacima pišite na hello@dannwebworkshop.rs.

Koje podatke prikupljamo

Kada popunite kontakt formu na ovom sajtu, prikupljamo samo ono što nam sami pošaljete:

  • Ime
  • Email adresu
  • Telefon (ako ga unesete — nije obavezno)
  • Tip sajta koji Vas zanima
  • Sadržaj poruke koju napišete

Osnov za obradu

Podatke obrađujemo na osnovu Vašeg pristanka, koji dajete označavanjem polja za saglasnost pre slanja forme. Pristanak možete povući u bilo kom trenutku, slanjem mejla na adresu iznad.

Kako koristimo Vaše podatke

Vaš email i ostali podaci koriste se isključivo da bismo Vam odgovorili na upit koji ste poslali. To je sve.

Ne koristimo Vaš email za slanje reklama, newslettera, ili bilo šta osim odgovora na Vašu poruku. Vaše podatke ne prodajemo, ne iznajmljujemo i ne ustupamo drugima.

Gde se podaci čuvaju

Poruke poslate preko kontakt forme čuvaju se u tabeli na Google servisima (Google Sheets) i stižu nam mejlom, pa ih Google tehnički obrađuje kao pružalac usluge. Pristup tim podacima imamo samo mi. Ne koristimo servise za marketing ili analitiku Vaših ličnih podataka.

Fontovi na sajtu učitavaju se sa Google servera (Google Fonts), pa Google pri tome vidi IP adresu Vašeg uređaja.

Koliko dugo čuvamo podatke

Poruke čuvamo dok su relevantne za komunikaciju sa Vama. Ako želite da Vaši podaci budu obrisani, pošaljite nam zahtev mejlom.

Kolačići (cookies)

Ovaj sajt ne koristi kolačiće za praćenje ili reklamiranje. Pregledač pamti samo jezik koji ste izabrali (SR/EN), i to ostaje na Vašem uređaju.

Vaša prava

U svakom trenutku možete da tražite:

  • Uvid u podatke koje imamo o Vama
  • Ispravku netačnih podataka
  • Brisanje Vaših podataka
  • Povlačenje pristanka

Ako smatrate da Vaši podaci nisu obrađeni u skladu sa zakonom, imate pravo da podnesete pritužbu Povereniku za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti (poverenik.rs).

Pitanja o privatnosti?
Pišite nam na hello@dannwebworkshop.rs — odgovaramo lično, bez posrednika.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

DANN.web.workshop respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect through the contact form, why, and how we use it — simply, without legal jargon.

Who collects the data

Your data is collected and managed by DANN.web.workshop (dannwebworkshop.rs). For any questions about your data, email hello@dannwebworkshop.rs.

What data we collect

When you fill in the contact form on this site, we only collect what you send us:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if you enter it — optional)
  • The type of site you're interested in
  • The content of your message

Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box before sending the form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing the address above.

How we use your data

Your email and other details are used only to reply to the inquiry you sent. That's all.

We don't use your email for ads, newsletters, or anything other than replying to your message. We don't sell, rent or hand over your data to anyone.

Where the data is stored

Messages sent through the contact form are stored in a spreadsheet on Google services (Google Sheets) and reach us by email, so Google processes them technically as a service provider. Only we have access to this data. We don't use any marketing or analytics services on your personal data.

The site's fonts are loaded from Google servers (Google Fonts), so Google sees your device's IP address when they load.

How long we keep the data

We keep messages for as long as they're relevant to our communication with you. If you'd like your data deleted, just send us a request by email.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser only remembers the language you chose (SR/EN), and that stays on your device.

Your rights

At any time, you can ask for:

  • Access to the data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Withdrawal of your consent

If you believe your data hasn't been processed in line with the law, you have the right to file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (poverenik.rs).

Questions about privacy?
Email us at hello@dannwebworkshop.rs — we reply personally, no middlemen.
